# Typography

Interface text, headings and numbers use Chakra Petch SemiBold by the Chakra Petch Project Authors. The bundled source is `ChakraPetch-SemiBold.ttf`, licensed under the SIL Open Font License in `ChakraPetch-OFL.txt`.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/chakrapetch

`tools/create_readable_font.lua` draws the rasterized glyphs into the native `readable_font_atlas.aseprite` master, then exports the PNG atlas and BMFont metrics. The 64-pixel source glyphs support clear smaller text and larger score numerals. Font designs remain credited to their original authors.

The m6x11 atlas by Daniel Linssen remains in the card-art creation tools and existing Aseprite card lettering. Its source, master, metrics and permission text are retained in the project.
Source: https://managore.itch.io/m6x11

Pixelify Sans is retained as the earlier typography source under its bundled OFL license; it is no longer used for runtime text.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pixelifysans

Candy sprites, Joker illustrations, table and UI artwork are original. No Balatro game art or audio is included.
