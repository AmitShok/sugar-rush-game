# Typography

Large headings and score numerals use an Aseprite bitmap atlas of m6x11 by Daniel Linssen. The glyph shapes remain his work; rasterizing them into an editable Aseprite atlas does not make them original lettering. Godot loads assets/exported/ui_font.fnt and its font_atlas.png page exported from font_atlas.aseprite. Free to use with attribution; the supplied permission text is in m6x11-LICENSE.txt.

Source: https://managore.itch.io/m6x11

Body text and controls use Pixelify Sans Medium (weight 500), rasterized into `readable_font_atlas.aseprite` and exported as `readable_font_atlas.png` with `readable_font.fnt`. Word spacing is expanded for clarity. This font is by the Pixelify Sans Project Authors under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in OFL.txt.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pixelifysans

Balatro screenshots were used as art-direction reference: https://www.playbalatro.com/press-kit
All candy sprites, Joker illustrations, the felt shader and UI artwork in this project are original. No Balatro game art or audio is included.
